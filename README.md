## Yes, I Use GenAI to Co-Write This

Let's be completely transparent: **this site and its content are co-written with Generative AI.**

As an educator, lifelong tinkerer, and full-time manager of a 24/7/365 group, writing courses, treating AI as an academic taboo or a dirty secret would be completely hypocritical. Generative AI is here, it is fundamentally altering how technical work gets done, and pretending otherwise does students and practitioners a disservice.

Instead, I treat GenAI the way any experienced worker treats a high-powered tool: as a force multiplier for hands-on expertise.

## My Co-Authoring Process

1. **Ideation Grounded in Real Experience**: I come up with an idea, think through it, and try my best to frame out what I want to accomplish. Everything here is rooted in stuff I have personally done, know, and work with directly—real systems, real engineering toil, and hands-on operational realities.
2. **The Raw Brain Dump**: Because it's drawn from hands-on work rather than sanitized textbook theory, it often starts as messy notes, bullet points, terminal snippets, and thoughts compiled together.
3. **Processing with Antigravity**: I dump this raw collection into **Antigravity** and let it process and organize the thoughts into a cohesive, structured outline.
4. **Iterative Drafting & Collaboration**: Working from that outline, we co-write the content section by section. I steer the narrative, tone, and technical precision, while Antigravity helps scaffold the prose, format tables, generate code examples, and challenge my framing.
5. **The Human Filter & Verification**: GenAI models are probabilistic pattern-matchers—they don't have production debugging scars, operational context, or accountability. Every module, lab scenario, diagram, and technical explanation is steered, fact-checked, and validated by me before it ever goes live. No unvetted AI slop; only tested, functional material that actually works in the real world.

> **Practicing What I Preach:** In *GenAI for Tinkerers*, I teach students and practitioners how to strip away the vendor hype, move past basic web chats, and harness agentic tools pragmatically. Building, updating, and documenting this site alongside Antigravity is putting that exact philosophy into practice.
